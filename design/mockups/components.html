Every cell state, HUD piece and type style in CyberSweeper. Build from these. Colors come from tokens/palettes.json; nothing here is hard-coded to one palette.

Cell states

Hidden cells are raised, encrypted chips with one cut corner and a faint hex byte. Revealed cells sink into one continuous floor, so the frontier reads at a glance.

Numbers in every palette

Each row shows the eight numbers at default zoom and at the minimum zoom (0.5×), then hidden, flag and mine hit. Every number clears 5.7:1 contrast on its floor.

Motion

Motion answers the player. The board is the only thing that moves on its own, and only the cipher bytes.

Moment
What happens
Timing
Reveal
Cipher byte scrambles through 3–4 random bytes, then resolves into the number with a glow bloom.

Cascade
Cells flip in rings outward from the click. Each ring flashes the floor toward accent, then settles.

Hex bytes on hidden cells re-roll in a slow diagonal wave. Freeze it when reduced motion is on.
